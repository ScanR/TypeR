# Changelog

## 3.0.4

- The panel reports its own version correctly again. The 3.0.3 packages still carried 3.0.2 in the panel: Help showed the old version and the update check kept offering 3.0.3 to a panel that already had it, and on Windows the automatic install could fetch the 3.0.2 package back. The release checks now fail when the panel version differs from the package version.
- Windows: Photoshop's tool keys and the shortcuts of actions no longer die after a paste in the new TypeR. Since 3.0.2, every paste, style or TextShapeR change gave the keyboard focus to a hidden element of the panel, to take it out of the panel's WebView, even when the document already had it (a paste by shortcut). A UXP panel that holds the focus on Windows keeps Photoshop's keys for itself, so the tool keys stayed dead until a click on the document; the Legacy panel was not affected. TypeR no longer gives the focus to the panel: after a paste by shortcut it leaves the focus where it is, and after a click in the panel it asks TypeR Shortcuts.exe alone to hand the focus back to Photoshop's window. Not yet confirmed on a Windows computer.
- Multi-bubble no longer stores wrong bubbles when the Magic Wand (or a lasso, a marquee, the Object Selection tool) is on "Add to selection", "Subtract" or "Intersect". Each click was joined to the ones before, so every bubble was stored as the rectangle around all of them and the texts landed between the bubbles. TypeR sets the tool back to "New selection" while multi-bubble is on and says so; a click that was already joined to another bubble is left out, to be made again.
- The warning "TypeR could not safely read or save your data" can be closed, and now shows the file, the reason and the error code. It could not be removed while saving kept failing, as on a Windows computer where an antivirus or a read-only attribute refuses the replacement of the settings file: TypeR then writes the file in place, checked after writing, and the `.bak` copy stays as the safety net. A settings file that is corrupt with no valid backup is kept aside (`.corrupt-…`) and saving resumes, instead of every save being refused until the restart. A file the Legacy panel's own reader refuses is read with Node before being called unreadable. **Retry saving** saves the current state again. The backup copy is refreshed at most once a minute instead of at every save, which halves the disk work of the autosave while typing.
- Smart paste: a double bubble against the frame of its panel is split at the first click. Two bubbles side by side or stacked against a frame show one notch only, where they meet, and the other end of their seam is on the frame: TypeR took them for a single bubble and pasted one line. It is the commonest double bubble of a manga page. Checked on 696 lettered pages of 35 series, with the Magic Wand's real outlines: 23 double bubbles more are split, each between its two texts, and no single bubble is cut (1,362 bubbles checked). A double bubble with a shallow notch, a tail right at its notch or a tilted frame still takes the second click.
- Paste on click: the second click in a bubble that got one line cuts a double bubble under a frame straight from its notch to the frame. It could cut across a lobe, to a corner of the frame, and place both texts badly. Undoing a one-line paste now shows the tip about that second click, once: click the same bubble a second time and TypeR splits it.
- Smart paste: a small bubble stuck on a large one is split at the first click too. With the frame rule above, 299 of the 347 bubbles of the test pages whose lettering makes two blocks are split at the first click, against 276 before; the others take the second click.
- Smart paste: a Magic Wand click whose selection leaks into the page is pasted around the click. A bubble open on its panel, a bubble drawn with strokes that do not meet or text set on the bare page went to the classic paste, which set the line on one row in the middle of the page-wide selection, often far from the click. TypeR now reads the selection around the click only: a bubble whose gaps can be closed gets its text like a closed one, anything else gets it in the free area around the click, clear of the drawing. The Legacy panel does it too: it had no open bubbles at all, and read the outline of the whole page before giving up. A manual Paste uses the click that made the selection. On 126 such clicks of the test pages (open bubbles and texts outside a bubble, cleaned), the smart paste now places 126, against 16.
- Smart paste no longer hands these bubbles over to the classic paste, which pasted the line at one size without fitting it: a click on the seam of a double bubble (1 double in 15 on the test pages: the bubble that reads first now takes the first line), a click on the very edge of a bubble, a selection with a very dense outline, a text much larger than its bubble, a line with spaces around it. In the new TypeR, a line whose punctuation hangs outside its text box (Photoshop's Roman Hanging Punctuation) was refused as badly converted, and reporting any refusal crashed since 3.0.3 ("PhotoshopError is not defined"). On the 1,373 bubbles of the test pages, clicked with the Magic Wand in Photoshop, every one is now pasted by the smart paste in the new TypeR (1,039 single, 320 double and 14 triple bubbles); the Legacy panel pastes the 357 tried.

## 3.0.3

- The new TypeR applies styles to a selected text layer again. In 3.0.2 it answered "No text layer specified" even with a text layer selected: the focus release, started as the style was applied, brought Photoshop forward while the style was being written and made Photoshop refuse the change. The focus release now waits for each change to finish, and never runs during one.
- The new TypeR no longer closes by itself when it is expanded from a collapsed icon dock. The focus release of 3.0.2 gave the focus back to Photoshop as soon as the pointer left the panel, and Photoshop collapsed that panel as after a click elsewhere, about a second after opening it. The release now waits for a click in the panel or an editor being typed in; a panel Photoshop only showed is left alone. Applying a style from such a panel still collapses it, as Photoshop does on the next click outside.
- The script keeps the line just pasted in view. When long lines wrapped over several rows, the five lines kept above it could fill the whole sheet and leave it hidden below; they now take at most half the sheet. A paste on the last line of a page linked to the next one also scrolls the script now.

## 3.0.2

- TypeR Studio: the new **Open in TypeR Studio** footer button sends the open Photoshop documents to TypeR Studio, with the script if wanted. Pending changes are saved first, documents without a file yet are saved as PSD in a folder you choose, and the panel offers the installer for this computer when Studio is missing. The button can be hidden in Settings > Appearance, like the other footer buttons.
- TypeR Studio: pages that Studio opens in Photoshop bring their script along. The panel makes the tab holding the same text current, or opens a new one. In the new TypeR, an opened document now reaches the panel as `documentAfterActivate` does in the Legacy panel.
- TypeR Studio: inside Studio, the font viewer installs the fonts it downloads through Studio's own font host.
- TypeR Studio is presented once in a startup window, when the TypeR site switches the announcement on, and only once no other window is open. It is never shown to a first-time user or inside Studio. Later brings it back a week on, Try it online and Download end it, and Don't show again blocks later announcements.
- Smart paste: the reading order of double and triple bubbles is a setting, manga (right to left) by default, comics and manhwa (left to right) as an option. The text direction no longer decides which chamber, or which text of Align, comes first.
- The text size keys repeat at once when held.
- Page headings in Markdown bold, as a Google Docs paste brings them, are read as page headings.
- The font picker stays open when a phone's keyboard comes up, and leaves the keyboard down on touch screens.
- The editor theme presets have retuned colours.
- The keyboard Debug tab is hidden from Settings: it only showed dashes outside the new TypeR.

- Windows Legacy installer: inventories with blank lines or trailing whitespace now pass both validation and staged-file verification. The staged files are checked against the already validated paths and hashes.

- Smart paste no longer shows "Smart paste unavailable": a text too long for its bubble, even at the smallest size and without margin, is now placed by the smart paste itself, centred on the bubble at the smallest size and overflowing it as little as possible (as it already did in each half of a double bubble). For the rare failures left, the classic paste takes over without a warning.
- Smart paste keeps paragraph text when Photoshop rewraps during conversion: it enlarges the frame and restores the ink position. A conversion that cannot retain the composed lines rolls back to the existing classic fallback instead of silently creating point text.
- Active formatting buttons remain visible in custom editor themes.
- TextShapeR can preserve manual repeated and non-breaking spaces, keeping the surrounding words on the same line. Script auto-scrolling can now be turned off in settings.
- WebP import is available in the Legacy panel on Photoshop 23.2 and newer.
- UXP releases passive WebView focus when leaving the panel, and releases a focused editor after a selection in Photoshop. Moving the pointer while typing, IME composition and dragging do not interrupt editing.

- Windows: when UXP's log confirms missing WebView storage, TypeR now starts a repair for the exact Photoshop/UXP binary verified in testing. This also works after direct CCX installation, CEP migration, older updaters and the Windows package swap: the repair ships inside every UXP host. Accept Windows administrator permission, save your work, close Photoshop, wait for confirmation and reopen it. The original DLL is backed up and can be restored. Unknown binaries are not patched; TypeR keeps its in-panel fallback. No Adobe sign-in or licensing functions are changed.
- The new TypeR opens even when Photoshop refuses it a WebView ("TypeR could not display its interface. No response from the WebView", `start=false, stop=false, bridge=false`). Photoshop 2026 (UXP 9.3) refuses every plugin's WebView when no Adobe user is signed in (Feature Restricted Licensing, offline or modified installations: "webViewDataStorage_ is not available" in its UXP log), and also when it has no WebView2 runtime it can start. TypeR then runs in the panel itself, without a WebView, within seconds of the refusal (at most 15 s when nothing else tells it): the same panel, script, styles, paste, settings and shortcuts. Differences in that mode: the script is edited in a plain field shown on click (the coloured lines come back when it closes), fields are drawn by Photoshop, and a few icons look slightly different. See `docs/uxp-webview-refused.md`.
- Windows: the new TypeR reads the keyboard with TypeR Shortcuts.exe again, the small program of 3.0.0 that Microsoft cleared of Defender's detection, instead of the native addon of 3.0.1. It also gives the focus back when a Photoshop tooltip takes it, and reads the Back and Forward keys of some mice.
- Fix the text pasted with Auto clean landing under the `TypeR Clean` layer, hidden by its fill, since 3.0.1: the text is made above the clean layer again, in the user's folder when that folder sits above it. A new text layer is created right above the active layer, as before 3.0 (3.0.1 brought every new text to the top of its folder, and 3.0.0 moved it by a computed layer index, which also raised a Photoshop error on some documents): it is brought to the front, with Photoshop's own command, only when the `TypeR Clean` layer sits above it and would hide it.
- A paste from Word, Google Docs or another editor with Markdown interpretation on no longer cuts a paragraph into several script lines, nor leaves a blank line between two paragraphs: the line breaks of the clipboard's HTML source (Word wraps it every 70 characters or so) are whitespace, as a browser shows them, and only the paragraphs and line breaks of the document end a line. The `*` and `_` of the document are no longer escaped either (`\*\*:` for a `* *:` style prefix): a style prefix pasted from a document starts its line, as in a plain-text paste.
- The script scrolls to the line just pasted again in the new TypeR: the sheet is scrolled by itself instead of asking the WebView to reveal the row, which it ignored after a paste.
- Multi-bubble with smart paste: the stored bubbles are selected again with "Sample All Layers", as paste on click does. The click was replayed on the active layer, the text just pasted from the first bubble, and read nearly the whole page: "the bubble is open" for every bubble after the first. When the smart paste of a stored bubble fails, the classic paste of that bubble uses the bounds stored with it instead of the live selection, which a failed paste had emptied ("No selected area."); a failed step also leaves no clean fill or half-made text behind, and the notice names a step Photoshop refused instead of "unexpected error".
- Ctrl+Caps Lock (or Caps Lock with another modifier) can be set as a shortcut again, as in 2.x: the shortcut field no longer ignores the key, and the keyboard readers of the new TypeR report it as CAPSLOCK.
- TextShapeR training accepts `.psb` pages next to `.psd`.
- The new TypeR no longer hides its WebView when it has not answered after 15 s: Photoshop 27.7 can take much longer to start it, and a hidden WebView never loads. The notice says TypeR keeps waiting, and the panel opens by itself when the WebView starts.
- Windows: when Photoshop could not create the WebView at all, which its UXP log records (no Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime it can start: the cause of the `start=false, stop=false, bridge=false` notices, reproduced by hiding the runtime from Photoshop), the notice now quotes that log line, says the WebView2 component is missing, damaged or too old for this Photoshop (an earlier one may still accept it) instead of asking to wait, and its help button becomes **Install WebView2**, which opens Microsoft's download page (restart Photoshop afterwards). Photoshop's own Home screen fails the same way on such computers. The notice also says where Photoshop looks for the runtime (its log names the folder), whether any WebView2 started in the session and whether the Home screen failed too, and lists Microsoft's per-user runtime folder with the others. The Windows installer warns, after installing the new TypeR, when no WebView2 runtime exists on the computer. The support build's collector reads the whole UXP log instead of its last lines, ends with a verdict in plain words and inventories the Photoshop installations and WebView2 components (folders, registry, policies, variables). A WebView that gives no sign of life while Photoshop's log records no refusal of it is asked for again, three times, before the notice (a user's Photoshop 27.9.1 refused TypeR's WebView 4 s after its own start, "webViewDataStorage_ is not available", while a reload later works); the notice counts those attempts. The support bundle's `3-WebView-Rescue.cmd` runs the candidate ways out one by one, closing and restarting Photoshop itself, and reads the result of each in the UXP log (French on a French Windows).
- Windows: a shortcut with Ctrl (Ctrl alone, Ctrl+Win, Ctrl+K…) can be recorded again in the new TypeR's settings. Photoshop keeps every key pressed with Ctrl from the panel's WebView, so the shortcut field never saw Ctrl while Shift and Alt worked; it now records what TypeR's keyboard reader sees, once every key is released.
- The new TypeR no longer stops on "the plugin host does not answer" at Photoshop's start, which **Reload TypeR** always fixed. The host answered each request of the panel within 50 ms, but while Photoshop starts its answer (about 1 MB: the panel's files and languages) takes more than a second to reach the panel, which only listened to its latest request and asked again every second: every answer arrived late and was thrown away. The panel now starts on the answer to any of its requests, and asks again less often after the first five seconds. The panel's files are also read once for all its requests, a reading that still waits after 15 s is started again, and the panel reloads itself once when it got no answer. A slow reading is written to Photoshop's UXP log with the step it waits on.
- Legacy (CEP) panel: the smart paste is much faster, every step of it being an ExtendScript round trip. A text that cannot fit at the smallest size no longer pays the whole ladder of sizes, paddings and hyphenation (a shout bubble with a long line: 214 candidate lines measured in 9 atlases and 86 searches before, 31 lines in 2 atlases and 39 searches now, the same placement), and when the estimated lines do not fit a size the panel no longer measures every candidate line of the text: the chosen lines are verified natively instead. The panel measures with whichever of the atlas (up to 24 lines converted to a shape and read point by point) or the single lines costs less on the computer, timed by Photoshop itself. The history check before each paste no longer reads every history state when nothing changed, a paste moves that reading forward by itself, a dense wand outline is read without its Bézier handles, and the script's language is detected once per script instead of once per paste.
- Legacy (CEP) panel: a Magic Wand click on the next bubble, while the text just pasted is still the active layer, no longer starts the inline TextShapeR outline read (a point-by-point path read that takes seconds on a spiky bubble) with the paste waiting behind it: a selection that does not touch the active text is not its bubble. Photoshop's events for the measurements of a smart paste refresh the panel once, after the paste, instead of reading the layer after each measurement.
- Legacy (CEP) panel: a text layer clicked while a selection made on another layer (the wand on the clean layer, for a bubble) no longer loses its selection to that layer. The reads TypeR makes in a temporary history state (the TextShapeR shape of the selection, which runs by itself on every such click, Align, paste on click, the multi-bubble capture) went back to the state before them, and Photoshop selects again the layers that state was made with: the clean layer came back within a second, TextShapeR asked for a text layer, Align and the user's own actions failed with "No text layer selected". Those reads now give the clicked layers back. The new TypeR was not affected.
- Windows: the keyboard shortcuts (tool keys, `Ctrl+T`…) no longer die after a paste on click or TextShapeR until the document is clicked. Photoshop's contextual task bar settles under the cursor with the new text layer, and the tooltip of one of its icons takes the keyboard focus, a Photoshop bug. The new TypeR's keyboard reader now sees that tooltip hold the focus and gives it back to the document at once. A click on a TextShapeR shape, on Insert or on a style also left the focus in the panel, where Photoshop receives no key (the Legacy panel passed them on): the new TypeR now gives the focus back to the document after each of those changes. The Legacy panel has no such reader: there, click the document or press the Windows key to get the shortcuts back.
- Windows: the tool keys no longer stay dead after a TextShapeR click, a paste or any other action in the new TypeR's panel until the document was switched. Photoshop's own ways of taking the focus back left it in the panel's WebView (a Microsoft Edge component, which passes no key to Photoshop, and which a click on the document did not always take it from): the panel now takes the focus itself, which hands it to Photoshop, and TypeR Shortcuts.exe, TypeR's keyboard reader, sets it on Photoshop's windows from outside if the WebView still holds it, as the Windows key does. The reader acts only on the panel's request, while Photoshop is in front, and never while a text field of the panel is being typed in. The focus leaves the panel as the change starts, not after it: a style applied to a TextShapeR layer took long enough for the next tool key to be lost.
- The new TypeR no longer fails with "NAPI API failure: String expected" on every paste with a style whose All Caps (or another option) was turned off in the style editor: that option, stored empty, is left out instead of being sent to Photoshop. The smart paste said "unexpected error" and the classic paste, multi-bubble included, showed that Photoshop alert.
- A script tag now sets the style of its own line only: the next line without a tag goes back to the style in use before it (main → SFX → main), unless another style was picked meanwhile or a default style is set.
- The arrow of a script line that is not the current one puts its text in the active layer without moving the current line. The arrow of the current line still moves on to the next one.
- A paste after a Magic Wand click made without "Sample All Layers" while a text layer was active (the text just pasted) no longer puts the text in the middle of the page: the wand had read that text layer and taken nearly the whole page. As paste on click already did, TypeR turns the option on, says so and makes the click again on the page before pasting.
- Windows: mice whose side buttons send the browser's Back and Forward keys instead of the usual mouse buttons 4 and 5 can now set them as shortcuts, in both panels and in the shortcut recorder.
- Windows installer: an inventory (`app/package.sha256`) with a byte order mark, Windows line endings or blank lines, as an unzip tool or an editor can leave it, no longer stops the installation with "Invalid package inventory", and when a line is really wrong the error says which one.

- Import the styles, profiles and settings of an installed CEP TypeR on first start, and keep the plugin's data outside Photoshop's version-specific folders.
- Keep the global shortcuts through a small keyboard reader, which also reports mouse buttons 4 and 5 on macOS.
- Read bubble and selection shapes from the selection mask, leave no history state for reads, and fix size changes on layers with floating-point colors, size changes on rich text and FontScanR on documents with a background layer.

- The installers start with a menu to choose the new TypeR (UXP) or the Legacy (CEP) version with the arrow keys. It finds the installed Photoshop and marks the version made for it as recommended. The release archive contains the UXP plugin, so it installs offline.

- When TypeR is already installed, the installers' menu shows the version found under each option and whether it will be updated, and the end of the installation says what changed.
- The archive now has only one file to run per system, `install_win.cmd` and `install_mac.command`. The other scripts moved to an `installer` folder, and `install_uxp_win.cmd` and `install_uxp_mac.sh` are gone: the menu or `-Uxp` / `--uxp` do the same.
- The standalone updaters (`installer/update_typer_mac.sh`, `installer/update_typer_win.cmd`) update the new TypeR when it is installed, instead of installing Legacy next to it, and otherwise offer it when Photoshop can run it. On Windows, a failed update no longer reports success.
- Once the new TypeR is installed, the Legacy panel is called "TypeR (old)" in Photoshop from its next start, and says at each start, before What's new, that the new version opens from Plug-ins > TypeR.

### Smart paste

- Paste fits the translation to the bubble's real outline: the style size is kept when the text fits and shrinks
  as little as possible otherwise, down to a minimum size (75% by default), the lines follow the bubble's silhouette, the ink is centred
  inside the outline, and one French or English hyphen is allowed only as a last resort.
- Black bubbles get white text without a stroke. A style can be marked as the Shout or Narration style of its
  folder; spiky bubbles and narration boxes selected with the Magic Wand then use it, unless the script line has
  its own prefix style. A rectangle marquee or a lasso keeps the line's style.
- Double and triple bubbles receive the next script lines in their other chambers, in one history state. Every chamber gets its line, centred, even if it has to overflow: the style size comes before the padding, which no longer applies along the invisible limit between two chambers.
- Automatic hyphenation follows the script's language instead of the interface's, and a word with an apostrophe can break ("quel-qu'un").
- Optional Auto clean paints the bubble under the text on a `TypeR Clean` layer (solid white or black only).
- Conditional refont rules give the text between two markers another style's font.
- A bubble too small for the padding keeps the text inside it: the padding gives way, then the margin. When the text still cannot be placed, the classic paste runs and a short notice explains why.
- Center after paste is now a setting of the classic paste only: the footer toggle is gone, and the setting is greyed out while smart paste is on (it still applies when smart paste falls back to the classic paste).
- Multi-bubble batches select each stored bubble again and paste it with the smart paste.
- Align knows double and triple bubbles: each selected text is centred in its own chamber instead of on the
  neck of the whole bubble, and a single text goes to the chamber it sits in (or the nearest one).

### Paste on click

- A Magic Wand click, a rectangle or a lasso can paste the current line at once (footer toggle and shortcut).
- Undoing a TypeR paste in Photoshop puts the script line back (live in UXP, checked lazily in Legacy).
- Clicking a bubble again right after it got one line splits it: TypeR undoes that paste and pastes the line and the next one, one per chamber, for double bubbles it did not detect (bubbles clipped by the panel frame, offset boxes).

### Shortcuts

- Shortcuts made of modifier keys only (Paste `Win+Ctrl`, Apply `Win+Shift`, Center `Win+Alt`) now run when you release them, and not if another key joined them. `Cmd+Shift+Z`, `Cmd+Opt+I` and every other Photoshop shortcut starting with these keys no longer run a TypeR action on the way, and a custom shortcut can extend them.
- The keep-size modifier works in any press order.
- Change the defaults Photoshop also reacts to: previous line is `Ctrl+Shift+Enter` (was `Ctrl+Tab`, which also changed document), next page is `Ctrl+Alt+Enter` (was `Shift+X`, which also swapped the colors), text size is `Ctrl+Alt+U` and `Ctrl+Alt+D` (was `Ctrl+Shift+Plus` and `Minus`, where Plus also changed the layer's blending mode), inserting the text is `Ctrl+Alt+L` (was `Win+Shift+V`, which also ran Paste in Place). Unchanged old defaults move to the new ones; customized shortcuts are kept.
- `=` and `+` count as the same key, so a Plus shortcut also works in Legacy, whose keyboard reader only reports Equal for that key.
- macOS: the UXP keyboard reader also reports the physical letter on layouts without Latin letters, so the default shortcuts work on them. Photoshop must be added to Input Monitoring in the system settings, or macOS hides letters, digits and symbols from the reader.

### Validation limits

Automated host tests simulate Photoshop operations. They do not certify every Photoshop/OS pair. Release acceptance still requires real CEP testing on CC 2015 and a current Photoshop version, on both Windows and macOS, including a long typesetting session. The existing video guide describes an older workflow; use the in-panel walkthrough for the current interface.
